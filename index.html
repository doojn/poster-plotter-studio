Zero-recalibration OpenBuilds workflow: calibrate your corner jig once, then just swap pens and paper!

The Zero-Recalibration Workshop Method: Tape an L-shaped corner guide on your plotter bed at machine (0,0). Each participant's paper pushes into this exact corner, so you never have to adjust home or calibrate between plots!

1. Physical Paper & Margin Lock

When locked, connected participant screens will automatically update to match this exact paper and pen limit.

2. OpenBuilds CONTROL Settings

🎛️ Pen Down Pressure / Servo Angle (S-Value) S500 (Balanced)

Controls how far the servo rotates down. Lower S = lighter touch (prevents crushing pen and protects servo from overheating).

📐 Pre-Plot Corner Verification Test 10-Second Test

Hovers the pen 5mm UP over the 4 physical sheet corners and 4 inner safe margin corners. Run once with a blank sheet in the jig to verify paper alignment!

🖊️ Pen Up / Pen Down Calibration Test 30-Second Test

Tests static nib pressure (3s hold), 3-5mm clearance gap (2s hold), 3 travel gap lines (checks for ink dragging), 4 staccato tap dots (servo timing), and a hatched box with crosshair. Ultra low-frequency lifts to keep your servo cool!

🪜 Pen Down Pressure Ladder Test (Step Test) 15-Second Test

Draws 5 test lines with increasing downward angles: S300, S450, S600, S750, S900. Run this to find your machine's exact ideal S-value without crushing your pen!
